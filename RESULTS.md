# Stationeers LN2 / -180 C cascade — calculation results

Generated by `calc_cascade.py`. Re-run that script after changing operating points.

## 0. What is being solved

- **Load:** cold bus at **-180 C (93.15 K)**, optionally tapping **liquid nitrogen**.
- **Sink:** room / box at **+40 C**, heat rejected by **pipe convection radiators** (any number on one pipe).
- **Machine:** each stage is `Evaporation Chamber` + `Condensation Chamber` + **Counterflow Heat Exchanger(s)** on the refrigerant path between them.
- **Energy unit:** **kJ per atmospherics tick**. `kW_SI = (kJ/tick) / 0.5 s`. In-game Watt is J/tick, so game-kW equals kJ/tick.

## 1. Constants used

- `tick_s`: 0.5
- `liquid_feed_L_tick`: 0.25
- `chamber_hx_m2`: 15.0
- `chamber_hx_J_tick_K_at_1atm`: 750.0
- `radiator_J_tick_K_at_1atm`: 52.5
- `cfhe_J_tick_K`: 750.0
- `cfhe_eta_per_unit`: 0.7
- `room_C`: 40.0
- `dump_loop_C`: 55.0
- `load_C`: -180.0
- `hx_derate`: Heat exchange scales with clamp(P/101.325 kPa, 0..1) on BOTH sides. Keep coupling loops >= 150 kPa (wiki) and ideally >= 1 atm.
- `evap_chamber`: 0.25 L/tick liquid regulator targeting 20 L (10%). Gas purge ~15 MPa*L/tick. HX area 15 m2.
- `cfhe`: Balances pressure / liquid fraction, then 48 sliding-window transfers at 15.625 W*dT*eta. ~70% temp swap per unit; daisy-chain.

Chamber HX throughput at 1 atm both sides: **750 J/tick/K** (100 W/m2/K * 15 m2 * 0.5 s).
One gas pipe convection radiator: **52.5 J/tick/K** (ThermalConvection 1.05 * 50).

## 2. Why most gases cannot be stage 1

Stage 1 must (a) still be liquid at ~+50–70 C under ≤ 6 MPa, and (b) evaporate colder than CH4 crit **(-78 C)** or N2 crit **(-83 C)** so the next condenser can actually make liquid.

**Coupling windows (K between previous freeze and next T_crit):**

- X → CH4: **21.68 K** (usable)
- X → N2: **16.68 K** (tight)
- Silanol → N2: **26.0 K** (comfortable, late-game)
- X → O2: **impossible** (O2 crit -111 C is below X freeze -99.8 C)

Pollutant freeze (-99.8 C) is below CH4 crit (-78 C) and N2 crit (-83 C), so X can condense those. O2 crit is -111 C — colder than X freeze — so X cannot condense O2. Alcohol/N2O/HCl freeze too warm to condense CH4/N2. Ozone/CO2 cannot dump at +40 C.

### LN2 at -180 C

- P_sat logT = **190.8 kPa**
- P_sat Antoine = **373.6 kPa**
- Wiki 100 kPa boil = **-198.1 C** (75 K). -180 C is warmer than that boil point, so LN2 at the target is a *pressurized* liquid: about **191 kPa** (logT), not 6 MPa and not a few kPa.

## 4. Per-stage heat budget formula

```
mol/tick     = 0.25 L/tick / V_liquid
Q_latent     = mol/tick * L
eta_CFHE     = 1 - 0.30^n          # 70% temperature recovery per unit
Q_parasitic  = mol/tick * c_p * (T_cond - T_evap) * (1 - eta_CFHE)
Q_useful     = Q_latent - Q_parasitic
Q_chamberHX  = 0.750 kJ/tick/K * dT * clamp(P_hx/atm) * clamp(P_ch/atm)
Q_radiator   = 0.0525 kJ/tick/K * dT * clamp(P_pipe/atm) * clamp(P_room/atm)
```

If `c_p * dT / L` is large (N2, CH4 spanning 100 K), CFHE is mandatory. One unit is not enough for the cryo stage.

## 6. Recommended schema (option C)

```
ROOM +40 C  (>= 1 atm)
  N x Pipe Convection Radiator  on ONE gas pipe
  dump gas: Pollutant or N2O, 300 kPa, loop ~55 C
        |  (chamber HX port, 15 m2)
S1 COND  Pollutant   70 C   ~ 3.8 MPa   (logT)
        |  gas up
     CFHE x2  (liquid down / vapor up, matched mol/tick)
        |  liquid down
S1 EVAP  Pollutant  -93 C   ~ 1.9 MPa
        |  coupling gas: Pollutant, 300 kPa  (below X P_sat, above 1 atm)
S2 COND  CH4        -83 C   ~ 4.7 MPa
        |
     CFHE x2
        |
S2 EVAP  CH4       -145 C   ~ 0.22 MPa
        |  coupling gas: N2 or H2, >= 200 kPa
S3 COND  N2        -135 C   ~ 0.7 MPa     *** LN2 TAP (liquid output) ***
        |
     CFHE x3
        |
S3 EVAP  N2        -185 C   ~ 0.16 MPa
        |  cold bus: Hydrogen >= 150 kPa  (will not condense at -180 C)
LOAD / extra N2 condenser at -180 C
```

Each condenser-evaporator pair is its own closed refrigerant inventory. Stages only exchange **heat** through the HX ports.

## 7. Heat-flow bottleneck ranking (recommended build, 1 chamber per end)

Steady state the whole cascade cannot move more heat than the weakest useful limit:

| rank | location | limit kJ/tick | kW_SI | why |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | ONE convection radiator @ 15 K | 0.787 | 1.57 | not limiting (N free) |
| 2 | S3 cryo | 3.392 | 6.78 | liquid_feed_useful_kj_tick |
| 3 | S3 N2 liquid feed raw latent | 3.592 | 7.18 | liquid_feed_latent |
| 4 | any chamber HX @ 5 K, 1 atm | 3.750 | 7.50 | area 15 m2 |
| 5 | S2 mid | 5.539 | 11.08 | liquid_feed_useful_kj_tick |
| 6 | S1 dump | 6.000 | 12.00 | chamber_hx_evap_kj_tick |
| 7 | S2 CH4 liquid feed raw latent | 6.250 | 12.50 | liquid_feed_latent |
| 8 | S1 X liquid feed raw latent | 12.500 | 25.00 | liquid_feed_latent |

**Practical bottleneck: S3 nitrogen liquid feed (~3.6 kJ/tick latent, ~3.2 kJ/tick after CFHE).**
Pollutant and methane can each move more heat than nitrogen, so extra S1/S2 chambers do nothing until you parallel **S3 evaporators** (or build a room-scale evaporator with a real liquid pump).

Radiators are **not** the bottleneck. At 15 K over +40 C they are ~0.79 kJ/tick each; ~5 of them dump a single S3's worth of heat; size S1's radiator farm to S1's own ~12 kJ/tick if S1 is used for other loads, or to the chain limit if this cascade is LN2-only.

## 8. Parallel / scale-up

- 1x S3 N2 pair: **3.39 kJ/tick** cold at -185 C.
- To match 1x S1 pollutant (~10.2 kJ/tick) you need about **4x** S3 N2 pairs.
- Room-scale evaporator (1x1x1 + active vent + liquid pump) removes the 0.25 L/tick cap; then chamber HX area or custom HX becomes the limit.

## 9. Build notes that eat throughput if ignored

1. **CFHE flow match.** Wiki: unmatched mol/tick destroys the temperature swap. Put volume pumps / regulators so liquid mass-flow ≈ vapor mass-flow.
2. **Evaporator liquid inventory.** Device targets 20 L. Below that, evaporation rate drops (RA2lover).
3. **Do not set cryo evaporator to 0 kPa** if it puts you under min-condensation (6.3 kPa for N2/CH4) — rate collapses and you risk freeze.
4. **Coupling-loop pressure ≥ 1 atm** or HX derates linearly. At -180 C that **cannot** be N2/O2/CH4 at 150 kPa without condensing — use **hydrogen** on the cold bus.
5. **Pollutant coupling** between S1 and S2 is fine at 300 kPa / -90 C (X P_sat is ~2 MPa there).
6. **Purge valve** on liquid lines between chambers to dump flash gas, or the liquid pipe hits 6 MPa.
7. **Insulate** everything below 0 C. Pipe convection to a warm room will steal the cascade.
8. Silanol option D is strictly better as stage 1 (wider window, 15.625 kJ/tick latent) but is late-game / trader.
